About the Hotel

A fresh landmark for stays in Apo

Arena Continental Hotel brings a clean, modern silhouette to the Apo skyline — a secure, gated property with private parking, tiled balconies and a calm residential setting just off Nnanna Ukaegbu Street, minutes from the High Court of the Federal Capital Territory and Apo's main commercial strip.

Whether you're in Abuja for business, a short city break, or need a serviced apartment for an extended stay, the property is built around comfort, security and straightforward, attentive service.

  • Gated, secured compound with on-site parking
  • Hotel rooms & serviced apartment options
  • Minutes from Apo Resettlement, hospitals & the High Court FCT
  • 24-hour front desk and support
